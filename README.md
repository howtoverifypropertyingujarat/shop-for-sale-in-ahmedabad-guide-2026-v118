### 5. Site Visits and Inspections

Once you have a shortlist of shops, schedule site visits. Inspect the properties for:

### 6. Negotiate the Purchase

After identifying a suitable property, the next step involves negotiation. Here are some tips:

- Understand the market value and be ready to justify your offer.
- Be flexible with terms like deposit amounts and timelines.
- Ensure that all discussions are documented.

### 7. Finalize the Purchase Agreement

After successfully negotiating the price, it’s time to finalize the agreement. Ensure that the contract includes:

- The purchase price
- Payment terms

### 8. Complete Due Diligence

Before making the final payment, conduct due diligence to verify:

- Title deeds are clear and free from encumbrances.
- The property has all necessary permits and licenses.
- The shop complies with zoning laws.

### 9. Close the Deal

The closing process involves transferring ownership. You will need to prepare necessary documents, including:

- Sale deed
- Transfer certificate
- Payment receipts

Once all documents are signed and payments made, you will receive the keys to your new shop.
